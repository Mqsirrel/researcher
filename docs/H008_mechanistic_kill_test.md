# H-008 Mechanistic Kill Test: Salivary Amylase (*AMY1*) CNV & Gestational Amylophagia

## 1. Verdict
**B. H-008 SURVIVES BUT IS WEAK**

*Rationale:* The biological link from *AMY1* copy number to total carbohydrate intake and altered oral starch texture perception is supported by human data (PMID: 40190144, PMID: 22523588). However, the specific leap from low *AMY1* to selective, compulsive craving for *raw, uncooked non-food starch* is biologically speculative and lacks direct experimental grounding. Furthermore, empirical obstetric data ($N = 2,368$, PMID: 20595493) demonstrates that gestational amylophagy is strongly associated with pregnancy nausea ($\text{OR} = 1.45$) and abdominal distress ($\text{OR} = 1.22$), providing strong support for the competing Upper-GI Acid-Buffering / Reflux Relief Model. H-008 survives solely because it makes a clean, low-cost, falsifiable genetic prediction (*AMY1* ddPCR) that can be definitively tested.

## 3. Evidence For and Against AMY1 $\rightarrow$ Raw-Starch Seeking

### Supporting Evidence:
1. ***AMY1* Copy Number Directly Scales Total Carbohydrate Intake in Women (PMID: 40190144, 2025):** Low *AMY1* copy number in women associates with significantly higher daily carbohydrate consumption ($p < 0.05$).
2. **Oral Chemosensory and Textural Transduction (PMID: 22523588):** Salivary amylase concentration dictates the rate of starch thinning in the mouth, altering oral mouthfeel.
3. **Oral Starch Sensing (PMID: 30684587):** Humans possess specialized taste transduction for starch hydrolysis products independent of sweet receptors.
4. **Slow-Release Glucose Kinetics (PMID: 42472050):** Uncooked cornstarch uniquely maintains stable normoglycemia over 4–8 hours in metabolic disorders without provoking hyperinsulinemia.

### Contradictory / Limiting Evidence:
1. **Reward Neurobiology Favors High Glycemic Index (PMID: 40467262, 2025):** Central dopamine release is maximal for rapidly absorbable, high-glycemic carbohydrates. Slowly digested resistant starches suppress appetite rather than driving cravings.
2. **The "Why Raw?" Contradiction:** Hypoglycemia should drive cravings for ripe fruits, bread, or sugar, which immediately restore normoglycemia. Craving dry, raw flour or cornstarch powder is an illogical metabolic response to hypoglycemia.
3. **Absence of Starch-Specific Craving Data:** Evidence does NOT currently bridge general carbohydrate intake to clinical amylophagia.

## 5. Falsification & Discriminating Predictions

H-008 would be **decisively falsified** by:
1. **Identical *AMY1* Copy Numbers:** An $N = 90$ ddPCR pilot on REVAMP DNA demonstrates identical *AMY1* copy number distributions between persistent amylophagy cases and matched controls ($\Delta \mu \approx 0.0, p > 0.50$).
2. **Directional Inversion:** Persistent amylophagy cases exhibit higher mean *AMY1* copy numbers than controls.
3. **GERD Medication Abolition:** Pharmacological suppression of gastric acid with antacids/PPIs terminates raw starch consumption in pregnant women without dietary modification.
