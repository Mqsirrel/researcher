# Citation-Level Audit: Deconstructing the "Dual Neural Circuit" Model of Pica

## 1. Circuit Audit & Arrow Classification

| Proposed Causal Arrow | Empirical Classification | Primary Evidence & Missing Links |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Iron Deficiency $\rightarrow$ Striatal $D_2$/$DAT$ Loss** | **DIRECTLY DEMONSTRATED (ANIMAL ONLY)** | Demonstrated in iron-deficient rat striatum (PMID: 11440625, 17150171); human PET/SPECT in pica is non-existent. |
| **2. Striatal $D_2$ Loss $\rightarrow$ Pica Craving** | **SPECULATIVE** | Zero rats in Beard's studies developed pica; extrapolating striatal disinhibition to non-food craving is unproven. |
| **3. Ice Chewing $\rightarrow$ TRPM8 $\rightarrow$ Locus Coeruleus** | **SPECULATIVE (UNTESTED)** | Hunt et al. 2014 (PMID: 25010620) measured reaction time only; never recorded LC activity or TRPM8 channels. |
| **4. Cold Mastication $\rightarrow$ Prefrontal Perfusion Boost** | **SPECULATIVE (UNTESTED)** | Cerebral blood flow / fMRI was NEVER measured during ice chewing in humans. |
| **5. Pregnancy Dysgeusia $\rightarrow$ Striatal Reward $\rightarrow$ Amylophagy** | **CORRELATIONAL / SPECULATIVE** | Focus groups (PMID: 24784797) report liking texture/smell; zero neurochemical/reward pathway proven. |
| **6. Toxins/Nausea $\rightarrow$ AP $\rightarrow$ NTS $\rightarrow$ PBN $\rightarrow$ Kaolin Pica** | **DIRECTLY DEMONSTRATED (RODENT ONLY)** | Established in non-vomiting rodents (PMID: 42025017); unproven and confounded in human pica. |

---

## 2. Claims That Were Overstated
1. **"Dual Neural Circuit Architecture":** OVERSTATED. Presenting these disparate observations as a unified, validated architecture was an illusion of synthesis stitching together rodent toxicology, rodent striatal biochemistry, an unmeasured psychological test, and focus group quotes.
2. **"TRPM8-Locus Coeruleus Prefrontal Perfusion":** OVERSTATED. This pathway has zero primary imaging or physiological evidence in pagophagia.
3. **"Striatal Disinhibition Drives Pica Specificity":** OVERSTATED. Striatal iron depletion causes general motor restlessness (RLS), but why dopamine disinhibition would select ice or raw starch remains completely unexplained.

---

## 3. What Actually Survives (Empirical Grounding)
1. **Pagophagia Rapid Extinction:** Pagophagia is tightly tied to iron deficiency and resolves within $<24–48\text{ h}$ of IV iron in humans.
2. **Cognitive Performance Boost:** Ice chewing acutely improves cognitive processing speed specifically in iron-deficient individuals (Hunt 2014).
3. **Qualitative Phenomenon:** Amylophagic and geophagic women describe intense organoleptic attraction to **texture (dry/powdery) and smell (geosmin/earth)** (PMID: 24784797, 38222205).
4. **Rodent Malaise Pica:** In non-vomiting rodents, visceral illness selectively triggers kaolin ingestion via the Area Postrema $\rightarrow$ PBN circuit.

---

## 4. Single Highest-Information Next Step
* **Direct Physiological Verification of Hunt Model:** Perform continuous bilateral Transcranial Doppler (TCD) ultrasonography of the Middle Cerebral Artery (MCA) in $N = 15$ iron-deficient pagophagia patients vs. $N = 15$ controls during randomized chewing of ice vs. room-temperature water vs. cold inert silicone to test whether cerebral blood flow velocity ($V_m$) actually increases.
