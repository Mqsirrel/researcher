## Adversarial Novelty Audit: H-006 (Olfactory Bulb & Desiderosmia)

### 1. Established Clinical & Neurobiological Facts
- **Geosminophilia & Desiderosmia in IDA:** In $N=280$ iron deficiency clinic patients (PMID 38222205, 2023), 34.3% had geosminophilia (craving wet earth scent), and **93.8% of geophagia patients had geosminophilia**.
- **Periglomerular Dopamine Sparing in Rodents:** Iron deficiency depletes Tyrosine Hydroxylase in periglomerular inhibitory interneurons, impairing lateral inhibition and prolonging odor exploration (PMID 29520328, 2012).
- **Olfactory-to-Pica Transition:** Case studies document brick smelling leading directly into brick eating (PMID 42152139, 2026).

### 2. Prior Art Verification
The core components of H-006 have already been proposed in the literature:
1. Desiderosmia in iron deficiency: Hansen et al. (2022, PMID 35260407).
2. Bulbar lateral inhibition loss in iron deficiency: Erikson/Felt et al. (2012, PMID 29520328).
3. Olfactory craving as an explanatory model for pica: J Eat Disord (2026, PMID 42152139).

### 3. Final Evaluative Classification:
**UNDER-INVESTIGATED BUT NOT NOVEL**
- The mechanism is physiologically sound and under-investigated with modern human neuroimaging, but the conceptual links are already published. It cannot be classified as N3/N4.
