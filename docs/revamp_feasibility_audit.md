## 3. Minimum Feasibility Query (Privacy-Preserving Aggregate Screening)
Before requesting individual-level datasets or biological samples, an aggregate feasibility query is required to establish whether the cohort contains:
1. **Cases ($N \ge 30$):** Participants in IV FCM arm with documented persistent amylophagy (baseline $\cap$ 4 weeks), post-treatment ferritin $>100\text{ }\mu\text{g/L}$, post-treatment $\text{Hb} \ge 110\text{ g/L}$, and a viable banked DNA aliquot.
2. **Controls ($N \ge 60$):** Participants in IV FCM arm with zero pica/amylophagy, post-treatment ferritin $>100\text{ }\mu\text{g/L}$, post-treatment $\text{Hb} \ge 110\text{ g/L}$, and a viable banked DNA aliquot.

## 4. Draft Official Feasibility Request

```text
Subject: Feasibility Query: Banked Sample Counts for Proposed AMY1 Copy Number Study (REVAMP Trial - ACTRN12618001268235)

Dear REVAMP Trial Steering Committee and Data Management Team,

We are conducting an academic research project investigating the genetic and metabolic determinants of non-food cravings during pregnancy. Following the publication of your substudy on the effects of ferric carboxymaltose on pica in Malawian pregnant women (PMID: 40368302), we have developed a hypothesis-generating project investigating whether salivary amylase gene copy number variation (AMY1 CNV) associates with the persistence of raw starch cravings (amylophagy) following iron repletion.

Before preparing a formal secondary research proposal, institutional ethics application, and Material Transfer Agreement (MTA) request, we are writing to conduct a brief, aggregate feasibility inquiry.

Specifically, we would like to confirm whether the REVAMP biorepository and trial database contain sufficient eligible participants meeting the following criteria:

1. Eligible Cases (Target N = 30):
   - Allocated to the IV Ferric Carboxymaltose (FCM) arm
   - Self-reported amylophagy (raw starch, raw rice, or raw flour consumption) at both baseline and 4-week follow-up
   - Confirmed post-treatment iron repletion at 4 weeks (serum ferritin > 100 µg/L and hemoglobin ≥ 110 g/L)
   - Available banked maternal genomic DNA/buffy coat aliquot

2. Eligible Controls (Target N = 60):
   - Allocated to the IV Ferric Carboxymaltose (FCM) arm
   - Zero self-reported pica or amylophagy at baseline and 4-week follow-up
   - Confirmed post-treatment iron repletion at 4 weeks (serum ferritin > 100 µg/L and hemoglobin ≥ 110 g/L)
   - Available banked maternal genomic DNA/buffy coat aliquot

We are not requesting any individual-level data, protected health information, or biospecimens at this stage. We are solely requesting confirmation of aggregate eligibility counts and sample availability to determine whether the proposed pilot study is methodologically viable.

If feasibility is confirmed, we will submit a formal secondary protocol for review by COMREC and the Institutional Data Access Committee in accordance with trial governance policies.

Thank you very much for your time, guidance, and assistance.

Sincerely,

[Investigator Name / Research Group]
[Institutional Affiliation]
[Contact Information]
```
