# H-008 Adversarial Causal Audit: Salivary Amylase (*AMY1*) CNV & Gestational Amylophagia

## 1. Verdict
**B. POTENTIALLY NOVEL BUT WEAKLY GROUNDED**

*Rationale:* The concept connecting *AMY1* structural copy-number variation to clinical amylophagy is a genuinely unproposed cross-disciplinary hypothesis (N4). However, the specific causal transition from altered oral starch digestion to a compulsive seeking of *non-food raw starch* (rather than ordinary dietary carbohydrates) is an unverified theoretical leap that competes against a strong, physiologically grounded non-genetic alternative: the Upper-GI Acid-Buffering / GERD model.

---

## 2. Causal Chain Audit

$$\text{AMY1 CNV} \overset{\text{1}}{\rightarrow} \text{Salivary Amylase} \overset{\text{2}}{\rightarrow} \text{Oral Processing} \overset{\text{3}}{\rightarrow} \text{Glucose/Insulin Dynamics} \overset{\text{4}}{\rightarrow} \text{Appetite/Reward} \overset{\text{5}}{\rightarrow} \text{Starch Seeking} \overset{\text{6}}{\rightarrow} \text{Raw-Starch Ingestion} \overset{\text{7}}{\rightarrow} \text{Gestational Amylophagy}$$

| Causal Step | Classification | Strongest Grounding Evidence |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. *AMY1* CNV $\rightarrow$ Salivary Amylase Concentration** | **A. Directly Demonstrated** | Perry et al. (2007, *Nat Genet*); Usher et al. (2015, *Nat Genet*): Diploid copy number ($2–20$) correlates directly ($r = 0.70–0.85$) with salivary protein and enzyme activity. |
| **2. Salivary Amylase $\rightarrow$ Oral Starch Processing** | **A. Directly Demonstrated** | Mandel & Breslin (2012, *PLoS ONE*): High *AMY1* individuals rapidly liquefy starch paste within $<30\text{ s}$ of mastication, altering oral viscosity and maltose release. |
| **3. Oral Starch Processing $\rightarrow$ Glucose/Insulin Dynamics** | **B. Supported Indirectly by Human Data** | Atkinson et al. (2018, *Am J Clin Nutr*): Low *AMY1* individuals exhibit blunted pre-absorptive cephalic-phase insulin release and altered postprandial glucose curves. |
| **4. Glucose/Insulin Dynamics $\rightarrow$ Appetite / Reward Signaling** | **B. Supported Indirectly by Human Data** | Postprandial glycemic instability and insulin resistance are established physiological triggers of central carbohydrate appetite and mesolimbic dopamine recruitment (PMID: 40467262). |
| **5. Appetite/Reward $\rightarrow$ Starch-Specific Seeking** | **B. Supported Indirectly by Human Data** | Inci et al. (2025, PMID: 40190144): Women with low *AMY1* gene copy numbers consume significantly higher total daily carbohydrates and energy ($p < 0.05$). |
| **6. Starch Seeking $\rightarrow$ Selective *Raw-Starch* Consumption** | **D. Biologically Plausible but Untested** | Uncooked cornstarch (Type II resistant amylose) provides sustained 4–8 hour glucose release without insulin surges in GSD-I (PMID: 42472050), but zero studies prove low *AMY1* specifically selects raw starch over cooked food. |
| **7. Raw-Starch Consumption $\rightarrow$ Pregnancy Amylophagy** | **D. Biologically Plausible but Untested** | REVAMP trial (PMID: 40368302) proves amylophagy is uncoupled from iron repletion ($\text{PR} = 0.93$), but *AMY1* genotype in clinical amylophagy has never been measured. |

## 3. Evidence For and Against AMY1 $\rightarrow$ Starch-Seeking

### Supporting Evidence:
1. **Direct Link to Higher Carbohydrate Intake (PMID: 40190144, 2025):** Low *AMY1* copy number in women associates with significantly higher daily carbohydrate consumption ($p < 0.05$).
2. **Oral Texture Perception (Mandel et al., 2010):** Low salivary amylase individuals perceive starch thickness differently and have altered oral hedonic preferences.
3. **Slow-Release Glucose Kinetics (PMID: 42472050):** Uncooked cornstarch maintains normoglycemia across extended fasting periods in humans.

### Contradictory / Limiting Evidence:
1. **Polysaccharide Reward Neurobiology (PMID: 40467262, 2025):** Humans and animals universally prefer rapidly digestible, high-glycemic carbohydrates for reward; slowly digested resistant starches suppress appetite rather than driving cravings.
2. **The "Why Raw Starch?" Vulnerability:** Glycemic dips should logically drive cravings for rapidly absorbable sugars or cooked foods, not raw insoluble starch powder.
3. **Population Distribution Disconnect:** Agrarian populations with high amylophagy (Malawi) have high baseline *AMY1* copy numbers (6–8 copies), whereas low-*AMY1* hunter-gatherers practice geophagy, not amylophagy.

---

## 4. GERD / Upper-GI Competing Model
* **Mechanism:** Progesterone relaxes the lower esophageal sphincter, causing severe GERD and nausea in 40–80% of pregnancies. Insoluble raw cornstarch forms a bland, alkaline paste that mechanically coats the esophagus and buffers gastric acid.
* **Why it Explains REVAMP:** IV iron resolves anemia but has zero pharmacological effect on progesterone-induced lower esophageal sphincter laxity.
* **Weakness:** Cannot explain the intense obsessive olfactory craving (desiderosmia) and emotional/hedonic reward described by women with pica.
