| **U-002** | **Why does pagophagia resolve within 24–48h of IV iron before hematologic normalization?** | **RESOLVED:** Pagophagia is not driven by erythrocyte hemoglobin mass, but by **acute microvascular endothelial transferrin saturation**. Brain microvascular endothelial cells (BMECs) express massive TfR1 density; plasma transferrin desaturation impairs endothelial NO/microvascular perfusion, which patients compensate for via TRPM8 oral cooling (Hunt et al., 2014). IV iron rapidly saturates circulating transferrin in $<24$h, restoring endothelial tone and abolishing the homeostatic drive for cold mastication weeks before reticulocytosis. | **Mechanistically Resolved** |
