# GI Competing Model Audit: Upper-GI Acid-Buffering vs. Correlation in Gestational Amylophagia

## 1. Verdict
**MECHANISTICALLY CONTRADICTED & EMPIRICALLY UNPROVEN**

*Rationale:* The "Upper-GI Acid-Buffering / GERD Relief Model" is physicochemically invalid and represents a correlational observation mistaken for a causal mechanism. While large cross-sectional studies ($N = 2,368$, PMID: 20595493) demonstrate an empirical correlation between pregnancy nausea and amylophagy, raw starch possesses zero chemical acid-buffering capacity and fails to form a viscous protective mucosal coating at body temperature. Furthermore, zero direct human evidence shows that raw starch relieves reflux or that pharmacological acid suppression reduces amylophagia.

## 3. Evidence For and Against the GI Model

### Correlational Support:
* **PMID: 20595493 (Young et al. 2010):** Cross-sectional survey of 2,368 pregnant women in Zanzibar found amylophagy associated with nausea ($\text{OR} = 1.45, p < 0.001$) and abdominal pain ($\text{OR} = 1.22$).

### Physicochemical & Clinical Contradictions:
1. **Zero Chemical Buffering:** Native starch ($(\text{C}_6\text{H}_{10}\text{O}_5)_n$) is neutral with no basic functional groups. It cannot neutralize $\text{HCl}$.
2. **Zero Cold Gelation:** Raw starch is insoluble at body temperature ($37^\circ\text{C}$); it settles as a granular sediment and does not form a protective mucosal barrier in gastric acid ($\text{pH } 1.5$).
3. **Temporal Incongruence:** Pregnancy nausea resolves by gestational week 16–20, yet amylophagy persists into late pregnancy ($44.4\%$ at 4 weeks post-treatment in REVAMP, PMID: 40368302).
4. **Subjective Motivation:** Women report intense hedonic craving for the powdery texture and earthy aroma of raw starch, not medicinal use as an antacid.
