## Cache policy

- Exact or near-duplicate questions should reuse existing evidence unless the previous search was incomplete or new evidence changed the model.
- Re-search only the missing dimension, new terminology, new date range, or unresolved contradiction.
- Do not repeat broad searches simply to obtain more papers.
