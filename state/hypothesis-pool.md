## Detailed Specification: Leading Candidate (H-005)
- **Mechanism:** Cellular iron depletion in the arcuate nucleus / dorsomedial hypothalamus activates Neuropeptide VF (NPVF) transcription, signaling through NPFFR1/2 to stimulate non-specific appetitive seeking and override cognitive food/non-food boundaries.
- **Trigger / Sensor:** Intracellular iron depletion sensed by Iron Regulatory Proteins (IRP1 / IRP2) or transferrin receptor-1 (TfR1) downregulation on hypothalamic tanycytes.
- **Pathway:** Hypothalamic NPVF neurons → NPFFR1 on LH orexin / NPY neurons → Ventral tegmental area (VTA) dopaminergic activation → Ingestion of textured/non-food substrates.
- **Testable Prediction 1:** Hypothalamic NPVF mRNA expression is upregulated in dietary iron-deficient rodents compared to iron-replete controls.
- **Testable Prediction 2:** Administration of a selective NPFFR1 antagonist (e.g., RF9) abolishes non-food appetitive behavior in iron-deficient animal models without suppressing general water intake.
- **Discriminating Observation:** H-005 predicts specific neuropeptidergic receptor blockade will cure pica even in the persistent presence of anemia, unlike H-001 which requires systemic iron repletion.
