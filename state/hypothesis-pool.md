## Adversarial Causal & GI Audit Summary

* **H-008 Status:** **B. SURVIVES BUT IS WEAK (N4, Confidence: 25% – 30%)**. Survives primarily as an oral sensory / texture-processing genetic modifier rather than a systemic hypoglycemia cure.
* **GI Acid-Buffering Competing Model:** **MECHANISTICALLY CONTRADICTED & EMPIRICALLY UNPROVEN**. Raw starch has zero chemical buffering capacity and does not gel in gastric acid at $37^\circ\text{C}$; nausea correlation (Young 2010) was mistaken for a mechanism.
* **Strongest Physical Alternative:** Oral Sensory / Texture-Reinforcement Model (oral mechanoreception + maltooligosaccharide taste transduction).
* **Decisive Next Step:** Dual genetic (*AMY1* ddPCR) and clinical symptom (longitudinal GI scores) query on REVAMP biobank cohort.
