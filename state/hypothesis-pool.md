## Detailed Specifications: The Two Novel Candidate Mechanisms

### 1. H-007: Hepatic Portal Vagal Transferrin Sensor Model
* **Observation Explained:** Why pagophagia/geophagia craving extinguishes within 24–48 hours of IV iron, before any brain parenchymal iron uptake or erythropoiesis occurs.
* **Causal Chain:** IV iron $\rightarrow$ rapid transferrin saturation ($TSAT > 80\%$) $\rightarrow$ hepatic periportal transferrin uptake $\rightarrow$ hepatic vagal afferent activation $\rightarrow$ Nucleus of the Solitary Tract (NTS) activation $\rightarrow$ acute suppression of parabrachial / lateral hypothalamic orexigenic drive.
* **Weakest Causal Edge:** Direct proof that hepatic branch vagal afferents alter action potential firing rate specifically in response to acute plasma transferrin saturation changes.
* **Decisive Experiment:** Selective hepatic branch vagotomy in iron-deficient mice: IV iron will extinguish pica in $<24\text{ h}$ in sham-operated mice, but will fail to acutely extinguish pica in hepatic vagotomized mice.

### 2. H-008: Salivary Amylase (*AMY1*) Copy Number & Gestational Counter-Regulation Model
* **Observation Explained:** Why raw starch craving (amylophagy) completely fails to respond to IV iron in pregnant women (REVAMP 2025 RCT, $PR=0.93, p=0.31$), whereas geophagy resolves ($PR=0.53, p<0.0001$).
* **Causal Chain:** Low *AMY1* copy number ($\le 4$) $\rightarrow$ impaired oral salivary starch hydrolysis $\rightarrow$ pregnancy-induced peripheral insulin resistance $\rightarrow$ selective homeostatic craving for slow-release retrograded amylose (uncooked cornstarch/rice) $\rightarrow$ persistent amylophagy regardless of iron repletion.
* **Weakest Causal Edge:** Direct empirical demonstration of *AMY1* copy number depletion in pregnant amylophagy sufferers compared to geophagy and healthy pregnant controls.
* **Decisive Experiment:** Digital droplet PCR (ddPCR) genotyping of *AMY1* diploid copy number in banked DNA from the REVAMP trial cohort: persistent amylophagy patients will exhibit significant enrichment of $AMY1 \le 4$ compared to geophagy and control groups (Odds Ratio $>4.0$).
