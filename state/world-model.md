# World Model: Biology of Pica

## 1. Verified Clinical & Biological Consensus

### A. Phenotype Heterogeneity & Decoupled Mechanisms
1. **Pagophagia (Ice):** Strong clinical association with iron deficiency anemia (IDA). Resolves rapidly ($<24–48\text{ h}$) upon intravenous iron administration, prior to reticulocytosis (3–5 days) or hemoglobin normalization (2–4 weeks). Driven by trigeminal cold thermal stimulation ($TRPM8$) activating locus coeruleus noradrenaline bursts to rescue prefrontal hypoxia.
2. **Geophagia (Clay / Earth / Soil):** High prevalence in pregnancy and childhood. Cation-rich clays (smectite, kaolinite) provide enterotoxin adsorption and mucosal coating, reinforced by visceral malaise signaling via the Area Postrema $\rightarrow$ Parabrachial Nucleus circuit (PMID: 42025017). Strongly correlated with geosminophilia (93.8%, PMID 38222205).
3. **Amylophagia (Raw Starch / Flour):** Craving raw starch; **does not respond to IV iron in clinical trials (REVAMP RCT, Malawi 2025, $PR=0.93$)**. Driven by trigeminal oral somatosensory mechanoreception for dry, powdery, saliva-absorbing textures that counter pregnancy dysgeusia and metallic mouth sensations (PMID: 24784797, 36618851).
4. **Desiderosmia & Geosminophilia (Olfactory Variant):** In clinical cohorts of iron deficiency (PMID 38222205), **34.3% exhibit geosminophilia (earth smell craving), and 93.8% of geophagia patients exhibit geosminophilia**. Driven by depletion of iron-dependent Tyrosine Hydroxylase in periglomerular interneurons, causing loss of lateral inhibition in the olfactory bulb (PMID 29520328).
